WORDPLAY ENGLISH WORD GAME

How to play

Guess the hidden English word in six tries. Each guess must be a valid word of the selected length.

CRTE
  • Burgundy: the letter is correct and in the right place.
  • Rose: the letter is in the word, but in a different place.
  • Gray: the letter is not in the word.
  • Repeated letters are scored one at a time.

Word-list attribution and license text

SCOWL size-80 US English words are redistributed with the notice below.

Frequency screening and ranking use wordfreq English Zipf estimates by Robyn Speer (snapshot through about 2021). The derived list is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0; changes made here: Zipf ≥ 1.0 screening, frequency re-ranking, and targeted editorial exclusions. wordfreq data includes Google Books Ngrams, Wikipedia, the Leeds Internet Corpus, OpenSubtitles 2018, ParaCrawl, and SUBTLEX (Marc Brysbaert et al.; freely available data). See the upstream README for full attribution and license details.

Company and brand names were cross-checked against public S&P 500 and Fortune 500 rosters plus a curated brand list. Dated and archaic forms were reviewed against English usage-label references including Wiktionary. These targeted exclusions are editorial, not a comprehensive entity or usage classifier; ordinary English homographs are kept.
